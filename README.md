## What I'm working on

### [CleanSlate](https://github.com/Today20092/podcast-bleed-reducer)

A desktop app for reducing microphone bleed in multitrack podcast recordings. Unwanted pickup from other speakers' microphones has bothered me for years in my media work. This is my attempt to make dealing with it easier.

CleanSlate processes audio locally and lets you listen to and adjust the result. I use it myself and guide its development through listening checks and feedback on the interface. It's currently an experimental alpha.

### [My FUTO Voice Input fork](https://github.com/Today20092/voice-input)

I use this every day on my phone. Built on [FUTO Voice Input](https://github.com/futo-org/voice-input), my fork adds more local speech recognition model choices and a less intrusive recording popup.

I wanted voice input to take up less space while I was using other apps. I also wanted to try models that worked better on my phone. Daily use helps me find the awkward interactions and decide what to improve next.
